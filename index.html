Reflection

Winter Note

“Perhaps the cruelest thing about winter isn't the cold. It's suffering in silence while knowing that nobody is coming.”

For HB S, winter is not simply a season. It becomes a metaphor for being human — calling for help with all your strength in a world that can sometimes be too quiet to hear you.

Philosophy

On Human Nature

Humans naturally want to understand and discover the world. Yet the ideas we create can eventually become the very things that imprison us.

People can be taught to see others as enemies because of politics, governments, ideology, borders, and beliefs — while forgetting the humanity beneath those differences.

Memory • Life

School Life

School is a place where people spend years wishing to grow up, only to later wish they could return.

Classmates, seniors, juniors, and teachers become part of memories through studying, joking, friendship, fun, and shared experiences. After graduation, everyone may go separate ways, but those memories can remain.

Academic struggle should never be a reason to humiliate someone. A person's worth is greater than a mark on a page.

About HB S

HB S is the author name of Mohammad Hasibul Hasan.

He is a writer, thinker, and novelist whose work explores the darker and quieter sides of human experience.

His writing looks beyond appearances to question why people think, suffer, love, fear, divide themselves, remember, and search for meaning.

Through fiction, reflections, and philosophical writing, HB S explores the questions that often remain hidden beneath ordinary life.

HB S Philosophy

“I don't write to make things just beautiful — I write to make you feel something and think about it differently.”

Every story can carry a question. Every silence can hide a thought. And sometimes the things people cannot say become the things worth writing.

Human beings are naturally driven to understand and discover. But sometimes the worlds we build inside our minds become stronger than the reality around us.

HB S writes about those contradictions — the distance between what people believe and what they feel, between what they say and what they leave unsaid.
